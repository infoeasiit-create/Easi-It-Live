# Easi-It Live Vision

Easi-It is a transparent social-impact platform that connects donors to verified beneficiaries through trusted vendors.

Instead of sending cash directly to individuals, donors fund verified needs which are fulfilled by registered vendors.

Funds are held securely by Easi-It (escrow system) and only released after successful delivery is verified.

---

## Core Problem
Donors want to help but do not trust direct cash donations.

Beneficiaries need support but lack access to trusted funding.

Vendors can deliver goods/services but are not integrated into donation systems.

---

## Easi-It Solution
We replace cash donations with verified fulfillment.

Donor → Easi-It Escrow → Vendor → Verified Delivery → Fund Release

---

## Core Promise
No funds are released until real-world delivery is confirmed.
