# Easi-It User Roles

## Beneficiary
A verified individual or group requesting assistance.

## Donor
Any individual, NGO, or organization funding verified needs.

## Vendor
A registered service provider or supplier who fulfills requests.

## Admin
Platform authority responsible for verification and escrow control.

---

## Key Rule
No donor funds go directly to beneficiaries.

All funds are held by Easi-It until delivery is confirmed.
